What we do.

Four services covering both sides of the same question — how organisations plan for the people they need, and how individuals plan for where they're going.

S/01

Workforce Planning

Getting the right people into the right roles at the right time, and seeing the gaps early enough to do something about them. We look at what your organisation has now, what it will need, and the most practical route between the two.

The output is a plan you can take to a board — not a report that sits in a drawer.

Typically includes

  • Headcount & capacity planning
  • Skills gap analysis
  • Succession & retention planning
  • Scenario modelling
S/02

Career Optimization

Individual guidance for people deciding what to do next — whether that's a first role, a change of direction, or a step up. We work through where you are, what you want, and the concrete steps between.

Practical and specific, not generic advice you could have found online.

Typically includes

  • Career pathway mapping
  • CV & profile review
  • Interview preparation
  • Offer & negotiation guidance
S/03

Mathematics, Finance & Statistics Advising

Support with the quantitative work people most often get stuck on. We meet you at whatever level you're working at and build from there, rather than talking over your head or under it.

Suitable for students, career changers and professionals who need to be confident with the numbers.

Typically includes

  • Quantitative coursework support
  • Statistical methods & analysis
  • Finance & modelling fundamentals
  • Exam and assessment preparation
S/04

English & Academic Advising

Help with writing clearly and navigating academic requirements — from structuring an argument to meeting the standards a particular institution expects.

Covers coursework, longer research writing, and the applications that depend on writing well.

Typically includes

  • Academic writing & editing
  • Research & study skills
  • Applications & personal statements
  • Language support
