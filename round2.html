One clarification

On "B for ease"

  • All three original concepts are identical to build and to edit. Same page structure, same blocks, same admin experience. So if ease of updating was driving the choice, it shouldn't narrow the field — please pick on looks alone.
  • Where B genuinely does win is page weight. It has no large background gradients or blur effects, so it's the lightest and fastest of the three. That's a real, permanent advantage for SEO and mobile.
  • The grid is not decorative. It's the layout system the whole page snaps to, which is what makes adding a fifth service or a new section later look deliberate rather than bolted on.
